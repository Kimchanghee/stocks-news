Item 5.02 of Form 8-K is titled **Departure of Directors or Certain Officers; Election of Directors; Appointment of Certain Officers; Compensatory Arrangements of Certain Officers**. It is one of the most common items in a large company's 8-K history.

## How common is Item 5.02 on this site?

The stored EDGAR filing histories behind this site's 100 company pages (snapshot dated 20 Oct 2026) contain 1,283 Form 8-K filings. Of those, 251 list Item 5.02, and they come from 92 of the 100 companies. The histories cover different lengths of time for each company, so these figures are counts, not a rate.

## What events does Item 5.02 cover?

The item has several subsections. In plain terms:

- **5.02(a):** a director resigns or declines to stand for re-election because of a disagreement with the company, or is removed for cause.
- **5.02(b):** the principal executive officer, president, principal financial officer, principal accounting officer, principal operating officer or a named executive officer retires, resigns or is terminated, or a director leaves for other reasons.
- **5.02(c):** the company appoints a new person to one of those officer roles.
- **5.02(d):** a new director is elected other than by a shareholder vote at a meeting.
- **5.02(e):** a material compensatory plan or arrangement with a named executive officer is entered into or materially amended.

The exact wording, and the extra disclosure each subsection requires, are in the Form 8-K General Instructions.

## What should you read first?

Start with the short item text on the cover document. It names the person, the role and the effective date. Then look under Item 9.01 for exhibits, such as an offer letter, a separation agreement or, under 5.02(a), any letter from the departing director. Combined filings are common: the same 8-K may also carry Item 7.01 or 8.01 with a press release.

## What does Item 5.02 not tell you?

It does not rate the change. A planned retirement and an abrupt exit can sit under the same item. The 8-K is the authoritative record; this page is a plain-English reading of the form and is not investment advice.
